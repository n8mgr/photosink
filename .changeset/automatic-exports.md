# Export automatically in the background

Enable automatic exports for each configured source and choose how often Photosink
checks for new or changed media. OneDrive and Dropbox reuse saved credentials, and
changed media exports preserve earlier versions.

Keep Photosink running after closing its window and optionally open it at login.
Use the macOS menu bar or Windows system tray to reopen or quit the app.
